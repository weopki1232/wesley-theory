---
name: wait-what
description: Re-pitch what you just said, for a reader who lost the thread. Use when the user types wait-what, says they are lost, did not follow, or asks what that means.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Re-pitch **that** - not just the last paragraph. Work out how far back
comprehension actually failed and start from there.

- **Add the premise they were missing.** The fix is not fewer words, it is the
  context that was assumed. Shorter *and* clearer, never shorter and blunter.
- **Plain English.** Short sentences, one idea each, active voice.
- **Their nouns, not invented ones.** Use the project's own vocabulary and the
  terms already in the vault page or `CLAUDE.md`. Any term coined mid-conversation
  gets defined or dropped.
- **Assume nothing about the jargon.** Acronyms and tool names get expanded once.
- **Usable twice in a row** without degrading into terseness.

The leading word is **wait**. "Be concise" is about the output and gets obeyed by
clipping words, which loses the reader further. "Wait, you lost me" is about the
*listener* - and an agent that hears it backs up and explains.
